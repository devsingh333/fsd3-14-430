### ChatGPT

ChatGPT is an AI-powered conversational assistant developed by OpenAI. It can understand natural language and generate human-like responses to questions and instructions. It can help users with tasks such as learning, writing, coding, brainstorming, summarizing information, and solving problems.

ChatGPT uses advanced language models to understand the context of a conversation and produce relevant responses. It is designed to make interacting with AI simple and accessible through natural conversation.
